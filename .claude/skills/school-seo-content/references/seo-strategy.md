# Strategy: pillars, personas, seasonality, competitors

> Rules live in the project-root `CLAUDE.md` (Bible); mutable facts in `seo/_registry.json`.
> This file is background narrative: WHO searches, WHEN, and WHY we win.

## The audience (who actually types the queries)

The searcher is never the student (ages 2–14) — it is the **parent**:

1. **Relocating expat parent** (EN queries, often from abroad): "international school
   paris", "english school west paris", "school for expat kids france". Time-critical,
   compares 3–5 schools, cares about: language support, fees transparency, waiting lists,
   commute from likely home areas (Saint-Germain-en-Laye, Le Vésinet, Marly-le-Roi,
   Croissy, Versailles). Decision window: Feb–Aug for September start.
2. **Local bilingual-ambition parent** (FR or EN queries): "école anglaise yvelines",
   "école internationale 78", "apprendre anglais enfant paris ouest". Wants English
   immersion without leaving the French context; often enters via camps/Wednesday
   activities first. **French-language pages are a user-decision item** (site currently
   EN-only) — until decided, capture this persona via EN pages that acknowledge French
   families + camps content.
3. **Camp/activity parent** (seasonal, both groups): "summer camp paris english",
   "holiday camp kids paris", "stage anglais enfant vacances". Lower commitment — the
   camp IS the school's discovery funnel; every camp page hands off to the program pages.

## Why we can win (the moat, made concrete)

- **Real-school specificity:** transparent fees on `/tuition` (many competitors hide
  fees behind forms), real term/camp dates, real staff on `/team`, real forest-pedagogy
  photos. Content farms and AI listicles cannot produce any of this.
- **Niche differentiation:** "forest school" + international school is a rare combination
  in Île-de-France — own "forest school paris" outright, and let it halo the bigger terms.
- **Small-catchment local intent:** head terms like "international school paris" are
  dominated by big institutions, but "international school yvelines / saint-germain /
  78" and "near me" queries are winnable NOW via local SEO (GBP + NAP + local content).
- **Age-band specificity:** "english preschool paris" (2–5) has different competition
  than "middle school" — three program pages let us fight three smaller battles instead
  of one big one.

## Seasonal content calendar (a school year is the editorial calendar)

| When | Search behavior | Content action (deadline = publish BEFORE the wave) |
|---|---|---|
| Sep–Oct | new-year settling, open days, "school year calendar france" | welcome/news posts, open-day announcements, October camp content (by early Sep) |
| Nov–Jan | early relocation research for next Sep; February camp queries | admissions guides, "moving to Paris with children" content; Feb camp posts by Dec |
| Feb–Apr | peak admissions comparison; Easter + summer camp queries begin | evaluation-layer guides live; **summer camp content live by February** |
| May–Aug | late admissions ("places available September"), summer camp peak | availability posts, camp logistics posts, next-year admissions refresh |

The loop treats an approaching season with missing content as a P1-priority gap.

## Competitive landscape (verify live before citing — do NOT trust this list as current)

Big-name competitors (British School of Paris, American School of Paris, International
School of Paris, Marymount, Ermitage, EIB) win the broad "international school paris"
SERP on domain authority. Our fights: local qualifiers (west/yvelines/saint-germain),
age-band terms, forest-school terms, camps, and French-system-navigation content they
don't bother writing. Before targeting any term, check the live SERP: who ranks, what
page type (school site / directory / listicle), and what's missing that a real local
school can add. Directories (international-schools-database.com, expatica) ranking #1
= also a citation/listing opportunity (mode F user-action list).

## KPI recap (Bible §1)

Inquiries first (PostHog organic-attributed `apply_click`/`contact_form_click`/
`whatsapp_click`…), SERP share second, AI-citation third. A camp post that produces 3
WhatsApp clicks beats a guide with 500 visits and none.
